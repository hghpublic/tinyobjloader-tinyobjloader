== bulding experimental view app with C++20/26 fails due to deprecated allocator members

When building the experimental view app with C++20 or C++26, the build fails due to the use of deprecated allocator members in `tinyobj_loader_opt.h`.

C++17 Feature Removals And Deprecations::
https://devblogs.microsoft.com/cppblog/c17-feature-removals-and-deprecations/

Visual Studio warning macros for C++17 deprecated features::
https://mariusbancila.ro/blog/2018/07/09/visual-studio-warning-macros-for-c17-deprecated-features/

Remove use of deprecated allocator members and types

----
_SILENCE_ALL_CXX17_DEPRECATION_WARNINGS
_SILENCE_CXX17_OLD_ALLOCATOR_MEMBERS_DEPRECATION_WARNING
----

. https://cppreference.com/w/cpp/named_req/Allocator.html
. https://cppreference.com/w/cpp/memory/allocator_traits.html
